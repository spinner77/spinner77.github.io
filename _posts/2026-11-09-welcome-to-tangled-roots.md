Hello, and welcome to Tangled Roots, the blog of Beechview Crafts.

I have an allotment in Buxton with wonderful soil and a lot of brambles. I'm slowly turning it into a place to grow food, medicine, fibre and dye. Alongside the growing, I spin, dye, crochet, weave baskets and twist cordage, using as much from the plot as I can.

## What you'll find here

- **The plants:** how each one likes to grow, how it's been used in kitchens and medicine, and the folklore that comes with it.
- **The making:** baskets from iris and crocosmia leaves, yarn dyed with apple leaves, salves from calendula, and plenty of attempts that didn't quite work.
- **The wildlife:** what visits the plot, what helps and what hinders.
- **The time it takes:** I log the hours that go into each thing I make, from seed or fleece to the finished piece.

A new post goes up every other Monday.

## Unravelling

Each post ends with a question to think over. Here's the first one.
